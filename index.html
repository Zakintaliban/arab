Atlas Design notes

Drag to turn the map, scroll or pinch to zoom. With the map focused, arrow keys turn it and plus or minus zoom. Country names on the map are buttons that open each article.

The 3D map needs WebGL, which this browser has turned off. Every article is still available below.
