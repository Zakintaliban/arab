Many places come from later tradition, not scripture; each entry says which.

Prophets Atlas sets out what Judaism, Christianity, Islam and other faiths of the Middle East say about the prophets, side by side and without taking sides.

Map data: Natural Earth (public domain). Locations marked “tradition” are where communities remember a prophet, not established history. See About for sources and method.
